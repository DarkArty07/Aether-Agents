# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Participants must act respectfully and must not harass, discriminate against, or publish private information without permission.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports are handled fairly and with due regard for the reporter's privacy.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
