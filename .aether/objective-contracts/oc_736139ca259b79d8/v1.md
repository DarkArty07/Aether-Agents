# Objective Contract: Retire Qualification Lab and Telegram Monitor without replacement (#546)

## Owner Intent

The owner directs autonomous removal of the formal Qualification Lab and Telegram Monitor, including periodic Telegram reports, because their actual use does not justify their maintenance and overengineering. There is no replacement in this objective: retain Hermes native cron and all other functionality unchanged. The current instruction supersedes the earlier proposal for a thin Telegram notifier. The owning accepted decision is specs/lab-monitor-retirement/spec.md section 1 and revised DESIGN.md PD-75. Issue #546 represents this retirement; closed #252/#367/#407 remain historical, not revived implementation authority.

## Objective

Deliver one reviewed, deterministically verified source retirement of both subsystems, integrated through the repository's normal green PR/merge path. The resulting source and built wheel/sdist no longer expose or depend on Lab or Monitor, while RET-03 through RET-05 preserve the remaining product, test isolation and authenticated historical-release validation. Material design, preservation oracles and executable checks are owned by specs/lab-monitor-retirement/{spec,plan,quickstart,research}.md. Source acceptance is independently complete; it is not installed-runtime retirement, a new release or behavioral qualification.
