group's raw logs were not retained, so the row is **withdrawn as a measured group**; the retained
earlier-candidate log shows 10/10. The timing effects it was used to attribute are unchanged (§6)

Effect 1 — SQLite busy-handler probing vs a 10 ms window: the timeline diagnostic
(`diag_concurrency.py`) was re-run at the fixed head with a retained log
(`/tmp/ts382f-round2/diag_concurrency-72890d4830.log`, 4 publications of the same legacy
scenario with the 1.0 s append budget). The publisher held the lock in 0.083–0.183 s
transactions (21–22 transactions) and exposed 19–20 lock-free gaps ≥ 8 ms inside the
append's wait window, yet 2 of 4 appends still exhausted their **single** `BEGIN
IMMEDIATE` attempt and raised the product storage-busy error — the mechanism measured,
not inferred. The fixture's appender gets exactly one attempt because its connection
timeout equals its patience, and SQLite's default busy handler sleeps in 50–100 ms steps
by then — so it can miss those ~10 ms windows. The fixture budget is 1/20 of the product routine budget (`_WRITE_PATIENCE_S =
20.0`) and 1/60 of the transcript budget (`_TRANSCRIPT_WRITE_PATIENCE_S = 60.0`), where
the same retry loop gets many attempts across the same batch boundaries. **The recorded
10 ms yield and the batch limits were left exactly as designed**; if the oracle must be
reliably green at its scaled budget, that is a Morfeo constant decision, not an
implementer one.

Effect 2 — intermittent host I/O stall: in 1 of 54 instrumented publications one staging
batch's *transaction body* took 7.873 s of the call's 7.875 s (~60 ms/row instead of
~1 ms/row, transaction count still 21), and the oracle's budget test recorded one 1.868 s
call the same way. Both are consistent with a disk/scheduling stall on this host: the
publication makes no data-dependent decision that could occasionally cost 14–60× more
work, the same fixture is rebuilt identically every run, and the isolated budget
repetitions show 0.121–0.165 s (round-1 head) and 0.129–0.245 s (round-2 head,
`budget-30.log`, 30/30). Pre-fix, a stall of that class extended a transaction that
already held the lock for the whole transcript; now it is bounded to one batch.
