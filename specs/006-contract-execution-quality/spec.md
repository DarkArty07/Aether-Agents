## Intent and scope

Make the existing responsibilities explicit, teachable and verifiable from Morfeo's
intent/design through Supervisor's independent units to Implementer's behavior/evidence.
Reduce avoidable ambiguity, serialization and rework without equating more prose or
workers with quality. The owner specifically requests Morfeo's personal intellectual
authorship; downstream execution must not replace it with worker-invented design.

### US1 — A design that can be received without inventing intent

Morfeo supplies inspected project reality, observable requirements, material technical
design and runnable validation. Supervisor locates them without recovering a chat,
accepts proportional sufficient input, and returns absent/contradictory material design.

### US2 — Useful independent execution

Supervisor derives traceable units, resolves contract-supported shared execution choices,
explains actual dependencies and uses existing parallel capacity without violating
file-level independence, real gates or isolation. A coupled unit is not split for a quota.

### US3 — A verifiable implementation delivery

Implementer receives enough context to exercise legitimate local judgement, stays within
its unit and proves each acceptance obligation. Supervisor detects incomplete delivery
even when the worker's prose says PASS, and owns integrated acceptance and closeout.

## Objective requirements

- **CQ-01**: Deliver the three standalone package-owned procedures
  `objective-contract-design`, `supervisor-decomposition`, `implementation-evidence`.
  Each owns method only, has proportional examples, pitfalls and verifiable outcomes,
  uses portable project-relative references, and preserves canonical precedence.
- **CQ-02**: Focused SOUL amendments bind each role to the applicable canonical procedure
  and its required outputs without copying the full manual, changing roles, granting
  authority or hard-coding a per-project skill list.
- **CQ-03**: Contract readiness distinguishes structural validity from design sufficiency;
  requirements/design/evidence are discoverable and material uncertainties are resolved
  or honestly returned. Small objectives do not acquire irrelevant architecture.
- **CQ-04**: Supervisor's existing `tasks.md` is the only executable breakdown. Unit
  deliveries satisfy R7-FR-705b; coverage includes every assigned requirement and no
  unrequested implementation. No new Objective Contract or hidden task tree belongs to
  Implementer. The native decomposition root releases children when its handoff is done.
- **CQ-05**: Independence, prerequisite reasons and writable-file ownership are explicit.
  Available independent work can run concurrently; shared-file/actual prerequisite work
  remains ordered. No worker-count target, concurrency increase or invented dependency.
- **CQ-06**: Implementer evidence maps assigned acceptance to actual checks/results and
  inspectable evidence, preserving local judgement. Review rejects missing obligations,
  fabricated results and unit-success claims presented as integrated completion.
- **CQ-07**: Package and materialize these resources through the existing canonical skill
  allowlist and lifecycle, preserving fail-closed path/hash/ownership/collision checks,
  all-role preflight and private learned skills. No new loader, registry or schema.
- **CQ-08**: Observe behavior organically during subsequent authorized real work and
  record expected versus observed outcomes in #317. No synthetic Q1–Q7 campaign,
  test adapter, fixtures or model probes are required for current delivery. Structural
  tests, source installation or missing bug reports do not establish behavior quality.
- **CQ-09**: Adopt only the intended source resources after actual destination and
  ownership inspection, backup and readback. Preserve private skills, configuration,
  sessions, boards and active flows. No restart, provider/tool change or service cutover.
  Existing sessions may retain already loaded instructions; new sessions are observed
  through normal usage rather than artificial qualification in this session.
- **CQ-10**: Preserve immutable historical contracts, Objective Contract schema/actions,
  public runtime APIs, delegation authority, providers/models, concurrency and protected
  edges. No Hermes-core change or extra role/tool/daemon/evaluator platform.
- **CQ-11**: Reconcile affected canonical specifications, documentation, capability
  evidence, inventories and tests without claiming unexecuted qualification or activation.
  Report release impact/action/channel separately from source integration.
