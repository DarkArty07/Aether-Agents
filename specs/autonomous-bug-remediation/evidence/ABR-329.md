## Disposition

No deterministic product synchronization defect was demonstrated. The two assigned regressions were made deterministic as tests-only evidence (`unit_compatibility_impact=none`):

1. `test_rebuild_waits_for_open_reader_before_publishing_candidate` now signals from the actual `_ProjectionReaderLease.upgrade()` fence, checks the worker is still blocked with `Event.is_set()`, and uses a non-blocking shared-lock probe as a post-call oracle that the lease is exclusive. The test releases the open reader only after the fence is reached and verifies, through fault injection, that a no-op upgrade fails deterministically; no fixed-duration ordering probe or sleep is used.
2. `test_checkpoint_sink_uses_only_fsynced_active_evidence_without_rotation` disables the collector's background flusher for this explicit fsync/active-snapshot test. Its explicit flushes therefore cannot race an asynchronous flusher or rotate the active segment between durable-evidence reads.

The product implementation was not speculatively changed. Issue #329 is not closed by this unit.
