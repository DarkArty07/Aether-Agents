## 8. Residual risk and limits

- Recognition is deliberately exact and terminal: lowercase `[truncated]`, `...[truncated]`, `…[truncated]` after whitespace normalization. Case variants and other transport markers are not inferred; widening the agreed forms would be a design question, not a local choice.
- A value that legitimately ends in the literal marker is denied; that is the agreed trade-off versus allowing a truncated durable card.
- The literal coverage command from the card cannot execute in the default venv (inherited exact-Hermes dependency); the exact `coverage run` lane CI executes was run instead (section 4), and the terminal integration phase re-runs the full gate set with the Graphify component provisioned.
- Full integrated acceptance, runtime canaries and issue closeout remain Supervisor/terminal-integration responsibilities.
