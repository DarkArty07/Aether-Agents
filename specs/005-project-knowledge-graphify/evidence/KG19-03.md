## Background and Purpose

E01 qualification in KG19-02 executed twice on valid trials (Suite 2 `20260923_210140_edb6a3` and Suite 3 `20260923_210555_a153b2`). In both trials, substantive source exploration (`read_file` of code and ADR in Suite 2; content search returning operative values from ADR and implementation in Suite 3) occurred before `project_knowledge.query`. While the knowledge component was consulted and the distinction between specification and implementation was drawn correctly in the final answers, the orient-before-explore order required by KG-19/AC-2 was not reliably observed.

Acting on the design steward's direction (comment 15), this unit refines the wording of the two packaged resources (`src/aether_agents/resources/profiles/morfeo/SOUL.md` and `src/aether_agents/resources/skills/project-knowledge/SKILL.md`) to explicitly state the order: consult an available graph before repository content searches and answer-bearing source reads for requests to understand a bound project's architecture, dependencies, implementation, or documented decisions, and then inspect current sources to verify.
