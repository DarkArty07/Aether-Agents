# Vision

Aether should feel like an intelligent software studio: the user expresses intent at the product level and receives a coherent, maintainable and verified result without needing to manage agents, prompts, branches or implementation ceremony.

The system adapts its depth to the work. Simple requests remain simple. Complex work gains explicit contracts, specialist judgment and evidence without obscuring ownership or multiplying process for its own sake.

Long-term quality is measured against strong general-purpose agents using equivalent prompts and representative projects. Aether succeeds only when its product coherence, correctness, continuity and efficiency are at least as good—not when it merely performs more internal activity.
